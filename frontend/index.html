What ProjectSync AI does

AI Activity Extraction

Reads PDF site reports and pulls out activities, locations, progress figures, and reported issues - no manual re-typing of field data.

Schedule Matching

Compares every extracted activity against your planned schedule using activity IDs, names, and location, with a confidence score for each match.

Delay Detection

Calculates planned-vs-actual variance automatically and classifies each activity as on track, at risk, or delayed.

Risk Analysis

Flags equipment downtime, material delays, and dependency chains where one delayed activity threatens downstream work.

Human Verification

Low-confidence matches never get silently accepted - they're queued for a person to confirm, reject, or rematch.

Project Intelligence

A single dashboard showing planned vs. actual vs. risk vs. recommended action, built from real backend data.
